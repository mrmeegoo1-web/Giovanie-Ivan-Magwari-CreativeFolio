About

Giovanie Ivan Magwari

Graphic designer and multimedia creative based in the Philippines.

I'm Giovanie Ivan Magwari, a graphic designer and multimedia creative based in the Philippines. I hold a BA in Communication from Batangas State University and am NC II certified in Contact Center Services.

My design experience spans brand identity, illustration, event design, and motion graphics — shaped by hands-on work as a Brand Designer at HLAF, a graphics and layout contributor for the SPES Program, and a media and broadcast internship at 98.5 Cool FM.

I care about clean, purposeful design that communicates clearly and leaves a strong visual impression, whether that's a brand system, an illustrated piece, or a short motion loop.
